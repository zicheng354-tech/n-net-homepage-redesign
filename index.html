Do your products support 120 V / 60 Hz?

Switch and box models list AC 100–240 V, 50/60 Hz power supplies (for example QB3082SPGM-200). The mobile pole is specified at 220 V AC. [confirm 120 V option for the pole]

What are your MOQ and lead times?

MOQ and lead time depend on model and customization level. Tell us the model and quantity and we'll confirm in the quote. [confirm standard MOQ / sample & production lead times]

What warranty do you offer?

Which certifications do you hold?
